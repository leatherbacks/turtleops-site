Quick Start Guide

From download to your first observation in 10 minutes.

1

Download and Create an Account

Install TurtleOps from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Open the app and tap Sign Up.

  • Enter your full name, email, and a password (6+ characters)
  • You may need to confirm your email before signing in
2

Join or Create an Organization

After signing up, you'll choose one of two paths:

  • Join an existing team: Enter the invite code from your coordinator (e.g., STCROIX2026). You'll be added to the team with the role your admin assigned.
  • Start a new project: Enter your organization name and you'll become the admin. You can invite your team later.

Team coordinators: Generate invite codes from the Operations tab. You can set the role (volunteer, coordinator, or admin) for each code.

3

Configure Your Project (Admins)

If you created a new organization, head to Operations > Project Setup to configure:

  • Species: Enable the species your team monitors and set a primary species
  • Beach location: Set your coordinates for automatic weather, tide, and moon data
  • Nesting season: Define your start and end dates
  • Data requirements: Choose which photos are required, whether to require measurements (CCL/CCW), and your survey mode (walking or ATV)
  • Contact info: Add coordinator name and emergency contact for your team

Skip this if you joined a team. Your admin has already configured these settings.

4

Check In for a Survey

On the Dashboard, tap the check-in button. This starts your survey session and records:

  • Your GPS location
  • Current weather, tide, wave, and moon conditions
  • Session start time and elapsed timer

You must be checked in to create observations. When you're done for the night, tap Check Out.

Auto-checkout: If you forget, the app will remind you after 6 hours and automatically check you out after 8 hours (configurable by your admin).

5

Record Your First Observation

Tap the New Observation tab. Here's the flow:

  • Identify the turtle: Search by tag number or name. If it's a new turtle, a record is created automatically.
  • Tags: Confirm existing tags are still present, or record new/missing tags. The app checks for duplicates across your organization.
  • Behavior: What was the turtle doing? (nesting, crawling, in-water, etc.)
  • Measurements: CCL and CCW if required by your project
  • Nesting: Did she nest? Nest marked? Record the timeline.
  • Photos: Capture datasheet, turtle, tag, and injury photos as needed
  • Submit: Review and submit. Data syncs to the cloud automatically.

Auto-save: Your observation auto-saves as a draft every 30 seconds. If you get interrupted, find it in the Drafts tab later.

6

Invite Your Team

From the Operations tab, admins can generate invite codes for team members. Share the code and have them enter it during signup.

Good to Know

  • Works offline: All data saves to your device first and syncs when you have a connection. No Wi-Fi on the beach? No problem.
  • Dark theme: The red-on-black interface is designed to preserve your night vision during surveys.
  • Relayed sightings: Admins can record observations reported by other surveyors without needing to check in themselves.
  • Turtle alerts: Admins can create alerts for specific turtles (injured, research interest, do not disturb) that appear when any team member selects that turtle.
  • CSV import: Have historical data? Admins can import turtles and observations from spreadsheets.
