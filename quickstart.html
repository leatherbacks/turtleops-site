Quick Start Guide

From download to your first observation in 10 minutes.

Free vs Pro: TurtleOps is free for individual surveyors and small teams — sign in, check in, and log observations at no cost. Pro features (Bob the AI assistant, Beach Chat, daily morning digest, per-observation PDF email, web admin dashboard, AI name suggestions, turtle merge tool, fuzzy tag search) require a subscription — see pricing.

1

Download and Create an Account

Install TurtleOps from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Open the app and tap Sign Up.

  • Enter your full name, email, and a password (6+ characters)
  • You may need to confirm your email before signing in
2

Join or Create an Organization

After signing up, you'll choose one of two paths:

  • Join an existing team: Enter the invite code from your coordinator (e.g., STCROIX2026). You'll be added to the team with the role your admin assigned.
  • Start a new project: Enter your organization name and you'll become the admin. You can invite your team later.

Team coordinators: Generate invite codes from Settings > Team Invite Codes. You can set the role (volunteer, coordinator, or admin) for each code.

3

Configure Your Project (Admins)

If you created a new organization, the onboarding wizard walks you through the basics on first launch in six quick steps:

  • Beach location: Beach name, GPS, and timezone — powers automatic weather, tide, and moon data
  • Beach sectors: The parts of the beach your team surveys (e.g. North End, Center, South End)
  • Species: Pick a primary species and any others you also track
  • Naming convention: Decide whether your turtles get human-readable names or are identified purely by tag number
  • Bob & Beach Chat (Pro): Toggle on the team bot and chat tab, set Bob’s tone (professional, casual, or playful), and add a region description for his replies
  • Photo & measurement requirements: Choose which photos are required and whether to require CCL/CCW measurements

Every step is skippable, and you can revisit anything later from Settings > Project Setup — including options the wizard doesn’t cover (nesting season dates, survey mode, and team contact info).

Skip this if you joined a team. Your admin has already configured these settings.

4

Check In for a Survey

On the Dashboard, tap the check-in button. This starts your survey session and records:

  • Your GPS location
  • Current weather, tide, wave, and moon conditions
  • Session start time and elapsed timer

You must be checked in to create observations. When you're done for the night, tap Check Out.

Auto-checkout: If you forget, the app will remind you after 6 hours and automatically check you out after 8 hours (configurable by your admin).

5

Record Your First Observation

Tap the New Observation tab. Here's the flow:

  • Identify the turtle: Search or scan a tag — partial matches and hex/decimal PIT formats both work. If she’s already in the system, the slot you matched on auto-flips to Present. If not, a fresh record is created.
  • Tags (three-state inspection): Each slot (LRF, RRF, RFF, LFF) is Not Checked, Present, or Missing. Mark only what you actually verified — skipping a slot leaves the turtle’s record untouched instead of silently re-affirming stale data. Scan a barcode-printed PIT to record an applied tag without typos.
  • Behavior: What was the turtle doing? (nesting, crawling, in-water, etc.)
  • Measurements: CCL and CCW if required by your project
  • Nesting: Did she nest? Nest marked? Record the timeline.
  • Photos: Capture datasheet, turtle, tag, and injury photos as needed
  • Submit: Review and submit. Data syncs to the cloud automatically.

Auto-save: Your observation auto-saves as a draft every 30 seconds. If you get interrupted, find it in the Drafts tab later.

One-page PDF email (Pro): Opt in from Settings > Daily Email Digest > PDF for each new observation to get a single-page form-style PDF emailed every time someone on your team submits. Useful for archiving, printing, or sharing with partners.

6

Invite Your Team

From the Settings tab, admins can generate invite codes for team members. Share the code and have them enter it during signup.

Good to Know

  • Works offline: All data saves to your device first and syncs when you have a connection. No Wi-Fi on the beach? No problem.
  • Dark theme: The red-on-black interface is designed to preserve your night vision during surveys.
  • Relayed sightings: Admins can record observations reported by other surveyors without needing to check in themselves.
  • Turtle alerts: Admins can create alerts for specific turtles (injured, research interest, do not disturb) that appear when any team member selects that turtle.
  • Bob can act on your behalf (Pro): Ask Bob in chat to pin a message to a turtle, add a research note, or attach a recent photo — he’ll handle it. He’ll also be honest when something is outside his abilities and flag it for the admin.
  • Backfill historical observations: Admins can add historical encounters one at a time from the web dashboard, useful for getting paper datasheets into the system.
  • Data export: Admins can export observations and turtles as CSV from the web dashboard.
