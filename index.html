Before You Go Out

🌊

Beach Conditions

Real-time weather, tide status, wave height, and moon phase fetched automatically. Includes an overall Activity-to-Visit score so you know what to expect.

🔔

Team Notes & Alerts

Share important notices with your team - equipment updates, turtle-specific alerts, or survey conditions. Everyone sees them on the dashboard.

✅

Survey Check-In

One tap to start your survey. Captures time, location, and conditions. Tracks your session duration and links all observations to the survey.

In the Field

🏷

Tag Lookup & Verification

Search by flipper tags, PIT tags, or name. Matches current tags, historical tags, and additional tags. Flags new turtles automatically.

📍

GPS Location

Automatic coordinates for encounter start and nest site. Track nesting locations and beach sectors across seasons.

📷

Photo Documentation

Capture and categorize photos - carapace profile, injuries, nest markers, tag close-ups. Stored locally until sync.

📝

Comprehensive Data Entry

Nesting status, egg count, carapace measurements, body condition, injuries, sample collection, and detailed activity timing. Auto-saves every 30 seconds.

Turtle Profiles

📅

Encounter History

Complete timeline for every turtle - when and where they were seen, who observed them, nesting records across all seasons.

🩹

Injury Tracking

Document injuries with location mapping - boat strikes, entanglement, shark bites. Track whether injuries are fresh or healing.

🔄

Tag History

Track original tags, replacements, and additional PIT tags with full change history. Never lose a turtle's identity.

📈

Growth & Nesting Data

Curved carapace measurements over time, nesting records, egg counts, and nest depth - all linked to the individual turtle.

Team Operations

☁

Cloud Sync

Data syncs to the cloud when connected. Multi-device access with organization-level data isolation.

📊

Personal & Team Stats

Track your own survey contributions alongside team-wide metrics - total turtles, observations, volunteer hours, nesting rates, and recapture stats.

👥

Team Management

Invite members with codes, manage roles (admin, coordinator, volunteer), and track who's actively surveying.

📥

Import & Export

Import historical turtle data from CSV. Export observations for reports and analysis. Keep your existing data flowing.
